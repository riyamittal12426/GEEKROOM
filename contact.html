Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining GeekRoom

How does the society help in improving technical skills?

A tech society helps you learn new technologies through fun workshops and hands-on projects.You get to work in teams, solve challenges, and improve your problem-solving skills.Seniors and mentors guide you along the way, making learning easier and more enjoyable.

What exactly does this tech society do?

Hackathons & Competitions – to solve real-life problems through tech. Projects & Research – team-based projects that can go into portfolios or publications. Connects students with alumni, mentors, and industry experts. Helps in building strong resumes and LinkedIn profiles. Offers platforms to participate in inter-college competitions or national-level hackathons.

What kind of events, workshops, or projects do members get to work on?

Members participate in hands-on workshops on coding, AI, web development, and more.They collaborate in hackathons, coding contests, and real-world project building.Guest talks and innovation challenges further help them learn, create, and grow.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely! We have programs designed for all skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced developers. Our mentors provide personalized guidance to help you grow at your own pace.

Do you offer mentorship?

Yes, we have experienced mentors who provide guidance on projects, career development, technical skills, and personal growth in the technology industry.

Are seniors approachable and friendly?

All seniors in the tech society are friendly and easily approachable.They’re always ready to guide, help with doubts, and share their knowledge.Working with them feels comfortable and encouraging, like learning with friends.
